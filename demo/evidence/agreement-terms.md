# DisputeDock Demo Agreement

Agreement ID: dock-demo-80

## Parties

The client commissions the worker to build and deploy a wallet-connected project website.

## Escrow

The working Studionet demonstration uses 5 native test GEN. Test GEN has no represented cash value and is not USDC.

## Requirements and weights

1. Responsive design — 2,500 basis points
2. Five complete pages — 2,500 basis points
3. Working MetaMask wallet integration — 2,000 basis points
4. Delivery by the committed deadline — 3,000 basis points

Weights total 10,000 basis points.

## Acceptance criteria

### Responsive design

The layout must remain usable at 390-pixel mobile width, tablet width, and desktop width. Navigation and forms must not overflow horizontally.

### Five complete pages

The delivery must include five usable application areas: case desk, agreement creation, lifecycle actions, evidence lab, and protocol documentation.

### Working MetaMask wallet integration

The Connect Wallet control must call the injected Ethereum provider, display the returned address, detect the current chain, and request addition or switching to GenLayer Studionet when necessary. A decorative control does not satisfy this requirement.

### Delivery deadline

The worker must provide the committed repository and public deployment before the on-chain submission deadline.

## Review and dispute policy

The client may approve the milestone or open a dispute during the review window. If disputed, each party must submit public evidence locations and SHA-256 commitments. GenLayer must interpret the agreement and authenticated evidence requirement by requirement.

## Settlement policy

The worker payout percentage equals the consensus weighted overall compliance percentage. The client receives the remainder. The contract, not the frontend, performs the authoritative calculation.

One bounded appeal is allowed only with at least one new evidence digest. Original verdict history remains immutable.
