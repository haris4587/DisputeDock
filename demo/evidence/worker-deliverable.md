The layout includes desktop, tablet, 800-pixel, and 480-pixel breakpoints. Navigation collapses on narrower screens, grids become single-column, proof records stack, and form controls remain within the viewport.

## Wallet integration disclosure

The delivered demonstration intentionally records a defect for this adjudication fixture: the visible Connect Wallet control is present, but in the submitted fixture build its click handler does not call eth_requestAccounts. Chain detection and switching therefore cannot be reached from that control.

This is evidence for a partial-compliance test case, not a description of the current DisputeDock production frontend in the repository.
